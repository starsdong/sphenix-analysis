\section{Open issues}
\label{sec:open}

Ordered by importance; [decision] = needs the analyser.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Simulation} [decision]. PYTHIA \Dzero$\to K\pi$ embedded or standalone through the same tracking and
  KFParticle configuration, to obtain the \Dzero{} mass and width, the fraction and mass shape of decays stored with the
  wrong $K\pi$ assignment, and the efficiency of the skim and the selections of this note. Without it no selection can
  be optimised and no expected yield given.
\item \textbf{Weighted background model at all \pt{} and selections.} The origin of the broad enhancement is found
  (Sec.~\ref{sec:hump}, done). Apply the ($A$, \pt)-weighted control to the full skim at all \pt{} on SDCC and to the
  tighter selections; understand the remaining slope of the double ratio above 1.95\,GeV/$c^2$; a like-sign or
  mixed-event sample (production change) would give an independent check of the weighted model.
\item \textbf{Swapped-mass spectrum.} Recompute $m(K\pi)$ with the $K$ and $\pi$ masses exchanged from the stored
  daughter momenta and study both assignments per pair, possibly selecting the assignment by $dE/dx$ below 0.9\gevc.
\item \textbf{Momentum scale and resolution.} The calibration from the \Ks{} and $\Lambda$ mass study (separate
  analysis) should be applied before fitting a \Dzero{} peak position and width.
\item \textbf{Statistics.} The full sample enters only with \Dzero{} $\pt>1.5\gevc$; the full skim (\fullOut{} candidates,
  13\,GB on SDCC) can be analysed on the cluster at all \pt. The DST area has grown since the production of 2026-11-05;
  a top-up production would add about a fifth more data (estimated from the file count, not measured).
\item \textbf{PID calibration.} Run- and $\eta$-dependent $dE/dx$ calibration; extend kaon identification above
  0.9\gevc{} if the calibrated resolution allows.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Glossary}
\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item[DIRA] cosine of the pointing angle between the \Dzero{} momentum and the PV-to-vertex direction.
\item[\Ls] decay length divided by its uncertainty.
\item[$z$] fitted yield divided by its statistical uncertainty.
\item[pointing / non-pointing] candidates with large DIRA (selections a--f) / DIRA 0.95--0.98 (control).
\item[skim] second-stage selection of the production ntuples (Table~\ref{tab:cuts}).
\end{description}
