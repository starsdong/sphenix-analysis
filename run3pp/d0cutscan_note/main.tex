\title{\Dzero$\to K^-\pi^+$ cut scan and signal search\\
in the sPHENIX Run-3 p+p KFParticle \Dzero{} skim (ana581\_2026p005\_v001)\\[4pt]
\large Analysis note \texttt{d0cutscan}}
\author{X.~Dong (dongxs) \quad -- \quad draft prepared with Hermes}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This note documents a search for the \Dzero$\to K^-\pi^+$ signal (and charge conjugate) in the KFParticle
\Dzero{} ntuples of the sPHENIX Run-3 p+p track DSTs. The input is a second-stage skim (both daughters
$|\mathrm{DCA}_\mathrm{PV}|>60\um$, \pt$>0.5\gevc$, DIRA$>0.95$): \nTen{} candidates from the 10\% sample, and
\fullOut{} candidates from the full production of 2026-11-05, of which the \nFull{} with \Dzero{} $\pt>1.5\gevc$ are
analysed here. A hand scan of \scanN{} selections (pointing, decay length, pair DCA, $\cos\theta^*$, TPC $dE/dx$ kaon
identification, \pt) and a grid of \gridN{} selections, each fitted with a Gaussian fixed at the \Dzero{} mass, show no
narrow peak: the significances scatter around zero with an r.m.s.\ of \gridRmsFifteen{} (expected 1 for statistical
fluctuations only), with \gridPosFifteen{} selections above $+2\sigma$ and \gridNegFifteen{} below $-2\sigma$. The mass
spectra are not described by low-order polynomials, so the local significance at a fixed mass follows the background
shape. Dividing the mass spectrum of pointing candidates by that of non-pointing candidates (DIRA 0.95--0.98) removes most
of the kinematic shape: in the full sample this ratio shows a broad enhancement around 1.87--1.89\,GeV/$c^2$ in every
pointing selection and a much weaker one in the control selection. The enhancement is
produced by the pointing selection itself: for prompt random pairs the probability to pass it depends on how the momentum
is shared between the two daughters, and the momentum sharing is correlated with $m(K\pi)$. Weighting the non-pointing
control with the pointing probability measured versus momentum asymmetry and \pt{} removes the enhancement while keeping an
injected narrow peak; the remaining excess at the \Dzero{} mass is $\hthreeAEx\pm\hthreeAeEx$ candidates ($z=\hthreeAZ$).
No \Dzero{} signal is claimed.
\textbf{Limitations:} no simulation (expected mass, width and efficiency unknown), no momentum calibration, no
like-sign or mixed-event background, one mass hypothesis per track pair as chosen by KFParticle, full sample only
with \Dzero{} $\pt>1.5\gevc$, statistical uncertainties only.
Boxes: \textcolor{blue!60!black}{blue} = choice made and why,
\textcolor{red!60!black}{red} = caveat / open point,
\textcolor{orange!80!black}{orange} = needs a decision by the analyser.
\end{abstract}
