\section{Momentum-scale correction}
\label{sec:kappa}

\paragraph{Map.} \fpath{MomentumScaleMap/run79509to79516_20261102/kappa_lookup.csv} (from the momentum-calibration
study, file of 2026-11-02; md5 \texttt{\mapMdfive\ldots}; copied to \fpath{calib/kappa_run79509to79516_20261102/} on
SDCC). Its header defines the convention $(1/\pt)_{\rm corr}=(1/\pt)\,\kappa_{\rm curv}$ with
$\kappa_{\rm curv}=1+\varepsilon+q\,\delta$, and the column \texttt{kappa} $=1/\kappa_{\rm curv}$ as the \pt{} multiplier,
$\pt^{\rm corr}=\kappa\,\pt$. The table (\mapRows{} rows) is a grid of \mapPtN{} \pt{} values (\mapPtMin--\mapPtMax\gevc) $\times$ \mapEtaN{} $\eta$
values (\mapEtaMin{} to \mapEtaMax) $\times$ \mapPhiN{} $\varphi$ values ($-\pi$ to $\pi$) for each charge
(\fpath{logs/kappa_map.txt}).

\paragraph{Application} (\fpath{tools/pvfix/kappa_apply.C}). $\kappa$ is interpolated trilinearly in
($\ln\pt$, $\eta$, $\varphi$) for the charge of the track, with \pt{} clamped to 0.1--20\gevc{} and $|\eta|$ to 1.5;
the track direction is unchanged. The \Dzero{} mass is recomputed from the daughters' (\pt, $\eta$, $\varphi$) with the
stored mass hypotheses, once without (\texttt{D0\_mass\_rc}) and once with the correction (\texttt{D0\_mass\_kc}); the
pair \pt{} and the momentum asymmetry are recomputed as well. All stored quantities are kept, and the corrected ones are
new branches in a copy of the skim (\fpath{D0skim_pvfix2_10pct_kc.root}, \kcN\,M candidates, \chkKcSameN).

On the skim, $\langle\kappa\rangle=\kappaPlusMean$ (r.m.s.\ \kappaPlusRms) for positive and \kappaMinusMean{}
(\kappaMinusRms) for negative tracks. For prompt candidates ($L<1$\,mm) the recomputed uncorrected mass agrees with the
stored KFParticle mass: median difference \dmRcMed\mev, central 68\% half-width \dmRcHw\mev, \dmRcTail\% beyond 20\mev.
The correction shifts the \Dzero{} mass by a median of \dmKcMed\mev{} (68\% half-width \dmKcHw\mev) and the pair \pt{} by
a median of \ptRelMed\% (half-width \ptRelHw\%).

\paragraph{Validation with \Ks{} and $\Lambda$.} 100 \Ks{} and 100 $\Lambda$ files of the stock 10\% production (25 each
from runs 79513, 79515, 80908 and 81609) are refitted with a Gaussian plus a quadratic background (background first
fitted to the sidebands), for the stored mass, the recomputed mass and the corrected mass
(\fpath{tools/pvfix/kappa_v0check.C}).

The correction narrows the \Ks{} peak from \vKsAllSigRc{} to \vKsAllSigKc\mev{} (\vKsAllRatio) and moves it from
\vKsAllMuRc{} to \vKsAllMuKc\mev, closer to the PDG value; the gain is largest at \Ks{} \pt{} of 1--3\gevc. The
$\Lambda$ peak narrows from \vLaAllSigRc{} to \vLaAllSigKc\mev{} (\vLaAllRatio). In the runs outside the map's training
range the \Ks{} ratio is \vKsOtherRatio{} against \vKsTrainRatio{} in the training runs.

\caveat{The map was derived from vertex-constrained daughter kinematics of runs 79509--79516 and is applied here to
unconstrained tracks (noted in the map file itself). Runs 79509--79516 are part of the 10\% sample, and the \Ks{}/$\Lambda$
check in those runs is not independent of the calibration; the other runs are. How well the map describes \Dzero{}
daughters (higher \pt, different charge combination per mass) was not tested beyond \Ks{} and $\Lambda$.}
