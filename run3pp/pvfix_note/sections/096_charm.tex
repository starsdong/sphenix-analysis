\section{3-prong charm hadrons (stock PV, 10\% sample)}
\label{sec:charm}
\paragraph{Data.} The D$^+$, D$_s^+$ and $\Lambda_c^+$ candidates come from a separate production with the stock
KFParticle (no PV fixes) on every tenth DST segment of \chRuns{} runs (\chSegs{} segments, \chJobs{} jobs;
\fpath{output/charm10pct/}, macro \fcmd{makeCharm3} in \fpath{macro/Fun4All_run3pp_KFP.C}, \texttt{prod} cuts): daughters
$\pt\geq0.3\gevc$, $\geq2$ MVTX and $\geq20$ TPC hits, track--PV DCA $\geq50$ / 30 / 20\um{} (D$^+$ / D$_s^+$ /
$\Lambda_c^+$), DIRA $\geq0.95$, mass windows 1.70--2.05, 1.75--2.10 and 2.15--2.42\gevcc, D$_s^+$ via
$\phi\to K^+K^-$ with $m(KK)$ 0.99--1.05\gevcc. All three daughters from the same crossing as the PV.

\paragraph{Method} (\fpath{tools/pvfix/charm_search.C}): as for the \Dzero, with the 3-body analogue of the momentum
asymmetry, $f_{\max}=\max_i p_i/\sum_i p_i$, as weighting variable (20 bins from 1/3 to 1, $\times$ the same 7 \pt{} bins).
Offline: all daughters $\pt>0.5\gevc$, DCA as in the job, $L<2$\,mm (D$^+$, $c\tau=312\um$) or 1\,mm, and
$|m(KK)-1.01946|<10\mev$ for D$_s^+$. Window $\pm$20\mev{} around the PDG mass, background fit over 1.72--2.04,
1.77--2.09 and 2.17--2.41\gevcc{}; in the D$_s^+$ channel the D$^+\to\phi\pi^+$ window is excluded from the fit and
reported too. Stored and $\kappa$-corrected masses (the latter recomputed from the daughters, Sec.~\ref{sec:kappa}).
The in-job mass window acts on the stored mass, so the corrected spectra fall off at the edges of the range; the control
spectra do the same.

\paragraph{Results.}
\begin{itemize}
\item D$^+$: positive excesses in most selections, largest for all \pt, pointing, $\kappa$-corrected
  (\chExDpNoneKcAllP, $z=\chZDpNoneKcAllP$, $z_{\rm c}=\chZcDpNoneKcAllP$; with INTT $z_{\rm c}=\chZcDpInttKcAllP$). The
  data/prediction ratio (Fig.~\ref{fig:dplus}) shows a bump of about 5\% at 1.84--1.88\gevcc{} on top of a falling trend
  (read by eye). Model-calibrated, the significances are at most about 2.
\item D$_s^+$: no excess ($|z|\leq1.2$), and none at the D$^+\to\phi\pi^+$ position ($|z|\leq\chAltMaxNone$).
\item $\Lambda_c^+$: no excess with the stored masses; with the corrected masses and INTT the tight selections give
  $z=\chZLcInttKcHiT$ and \chZLcInttKcAllT{} ($z_{\rm c}=\chZcLcInttKcHiT$, \chZcLcInttKcAllT), not present without the INTT
  requirement at the same level.
\end{itemize}

$f_{\max}$ was not validated beyond the injection test. The expected yields are unknown (no simulation).}
