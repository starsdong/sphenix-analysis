\section{Open issues}
\label{sec:open}
\begin{enumerate}
\item {[decision]} Corrected-momentum search as the main analysis, and the selections to be fixed before looking at the
  full sample (Sec.~\ref{sec:resultskc}).
\item Background-model systematic: the window scan gives r.m.s.\ of the local $z$ up to 4 away from the signal; a
  model with a stated systematic is needed before any significance is quoted (Sec.~\ref{sec:full}).
\item 3-prong charm: a production with the PV fixes; simulation of yields; validation of $f_{\max}$ weighting.
\item Simulation: expected \Dzero{} mass, width (with and without the correction) and efficiency per PV version; needed
  to fix the window and interpret any excess.
\item Background-model accuracy: $\chi^2$/ndf up to \chiMaxNone{} and a residual slope in the tight selections; a
  systematic uncertainty for the window prediction is missing.
\item KF fit: \pt/$\eta$-dependent error scale; a \Ls{} cut equivalent to the other versions.
\item Momentum-scale map: a map derived from other runs (or unconstrained tracks) to test the \Ks{} improvement
  independently; check on a charm-like decay.
\item Runs not in the stock production (about 13 newer runs) are not processed.
\end{enumerate}
