  below 1\um{} for most candidates but has a 90\% tail of up to \cloPairManyNinety\um; the cause of the tail was not
  isolated.
\item The \pt{} and $\eta$ dependence of the KF error scale (a single factor 7 is used).
\item The momentum-scale map for \Dzero{} daughters (only \Ks{} and $\Lambda$ checked, Sec.~\ref{sec:kappa}).
\item Statements read by eye from figures: the falling $S/P$ in the tight selections (Sec.~\ref{sec:results}), the
  two-bin bump in Fig.~\ref{fig:kcT}, the normalisation of Fig.~\ref{fig:effects}.
\item The expected \Dzero{} yield and width (no simulation).
\item Crossing and pair-cut fractions were measured in two runs (pair crossings) and on the skim (pair cuts), not per run.
\end{itemize}
